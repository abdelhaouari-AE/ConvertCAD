PDF drawings back into real CAD.

ConvertCAD rebuilds the lines, dimensions, hatches, blocks, layers and text of a 2D PDF plan as editable DXF and DWG, on your own computer.

Features

The intent of the drawing, not just its strokes.

CAD entities

Real CAD, not exploded lines

True dimensions, blocks for repeated symbols, hatches, dashed linetypes and editable text, sorted into layers created from the colours and line weights of the PDF.

Scanned drawings

Scans become drawings

Lines, circles and arcs of a scan are traced into vectors. Its text is read by the built-in PaddleOCR model, about 50 languages (English, French and other Latin-script languages, Chinese, Japanese). More OCR models, such as Arabic, Cyrillic or Greek, can be downloaded from the app when needed.

Scale and review

Measure before you trust

The scale is read from the title block or set on two points. Measure distances with object snaps, lay the PDF over the resulting DXF to compare them, and read the conversion report for more details.

Output

DXF or DWG, your way

Convert the whole PDF, one sheet of it or only the area you select. Save as DXF or DWG, in one file for all sheets or one per sheet.

Interface

Your language, your theme

English, French, Spanish, German or Arabic, in a dark or a light theme. Switch from the toolbar.

Automation

A whole folder at once

Convert every PDF of a folder from the window, or script it from the command line: ConvertCAD-cli plan.pdf -o plan.dwg

Your drawings stay on your computer

Every conversion runs locally: no account, no upload, no tracking. Client and NDA drawings never leave your machine. You remain responsible for converting only documents their owner allows.

Open source, nothing hidden

The full code is public under the GNU AGPL. The portable version runs without installation or administrator rights.
