### 1. Local processing
All conversions run on this computer. No drawing, file or data is uploaded or sent anywhere. The application makes no network connection (only when running from the source code with the optional PaddleOCR library installed does that library download its models the first time it is used).

### 2. Your right to convert
You are responsible for having the right to convert and edit the PDF drawings you open (client agreements, confidentiality clauses, intellectual property of the original author).

### 3. Engineering responsibility
ConvertCAD is a drafting aid, not an engineering authority. A converted drawing is a reconstruction and may contain recognition errors. Always check:

- the global scale against a known dimension,
- the reconstructed dimensions (layer **DIM**) against the original PDF,
- the layer **DIM_RAW** (measurement texts that matched no dimension),
- traced content from scanned pages (layers **TRACE_\***).

Converted files must never be used for construction, fabrication or contractual purposes without verification by a qualified professional.
